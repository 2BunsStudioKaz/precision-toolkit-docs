# Render Presets

Render Presets applies reusable **Preview**, **Production**, and **Final** settings to Cycles and Eevee.

## Apply a preset

1. Choose **Cycles** or **Eevee**.
2. Select the **Default** domain or a custom domain.
3. Choose Preview, Production, or Final quality.
4. Review the exposed render, shadow, volumetric, denoiser, and compositor settings.

The quality presets recommend engine-appropriate sample counts, but Samples remains an independently editable control and is not saved in a preset domain.

## Custom domains

Create a domain when you want to save a set of Preview, Production, and Final values. Use the preset actions to create, save, reset, or delete a domain. The factory Default domain is read-only.

**Compatibility:** Cycles works in Blender 4.3 through 5.2. The modern Eevee preset controls require Blender 5.2; they are disabled on older supported versions.
